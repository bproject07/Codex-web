# Community Code of Conduct

We want a practical, welcoming community focused on improving open-source
software together.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect other people's privacy and security.
- Keep technical discussions focused and evidence-based.
- Accept maintainer decisions about security, scope, and repository safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private messages or personal, account, organization, or company
  information without permission.
- Posting credentials, terminal content, private paths, or security exploit
  details in public discussions.
- Deliberately disrupting issues, reviews, or project infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or
project. Report platform abuse through GitHub's reporting tools. Report
security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a
public conduct report.
